What SunPeek is

SunPeek is a free tool that estimates how many hours of direct sunlight a building wall gets under a clear sky. It is provided as is, without an account and without payment.

The results are estimates

Every answer is a calculation from public map data, not a measurement. It can be wrong, because:

  • building heights in OpenStreetMap are often missing or estimated;
  • trees, balconies, eaves, awnings and buildings missing from the map are not included;
  • it assumes a cloudless sky; real sunshine also depends on the weather;
  • map outlines, terrain data and the selected wall can be imprecise.

Each result shows a confidence level and a range to help you judge it. Please check the place in person, at the time of day and year that matters to you, before you rely on it.

Not professional advice

SunPeek is not a survey, valuation, daylight study or solar installation assessment. Don't base a purchase, a rental, a legal decision or an investment only on it. Solar energy figures are rough averages; ask a qualified installer for a real estimate.
